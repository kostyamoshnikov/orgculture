Production & promotion

I take cultural and arts projects from idea to audience

15+ years on stage, in circus and in theatre. I know the industry from the inside, at every level of it — from venue logistics to holding an audience's attention after the announcement.

I work with theatre and arts projects — festivals, productions, independent teams who want the same attention to detail the texts on this site are written with.

What I do

Producing

Concept through to launch: budget, venue, team, logistics, deadlines. I take on the organisational side so the production has room to stay creative.

Promotion

Social media, coordination with venues and partners, press and blogger outreach. Not one-off advertising — a sustained presence, from the announcement through to the archive after the run.

Websites

A dedicated site for a project or festival, when a single social media page isn't enough: programme, tickets, press kit and archive in one place.
