In brief

Konstantin Moshnikov is a producer who promotes cultural and arts projects, from concept to premiere and from marketing to logistics. He has spent more than 15 years on stage, in circus and theatre. Author of Organized Culturality.

Organized Culturality is Konstantin Moshnikov's personal space: texts about films, plays, music and people, plus production and promotion for cultural and arts projects.
